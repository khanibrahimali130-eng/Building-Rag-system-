# ClipRank Labs - Company Overview
Document ID: DOC-01 | Type: Company overview | Version: 1.4 | Effective date: 2026-01-15 | Status: CURRENT

## About the company
ClipRank Labs (Pvt) Ltd is a fictional software company founded in March 2024. It builds the SEO Clip Score Tool, a web platform where creators upload long-form videos. The tool automatically detects short clips and scores each clip from 0 to 100 for search and social potential. All information in this knowledge base is fictional and for classroom use only.

## Who uses the tool
Creators, small studios, and marketing teams use the tool to decide which clips to publish. As of the report date 2026-11-01, the pilot platform had 6 registered users, 10 uploaded videos, 42 generated clips, and an average SEO score of 69.8. The top clip was C021, with a score of 84.
