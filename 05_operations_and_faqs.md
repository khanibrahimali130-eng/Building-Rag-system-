# ClipRank Labs - Operations and FAQs
Document ID: DOC-05 | Type: Operations and FAQs | Version: 1.2 | Effective date: 2026-10-01 | Status: CURRENT

## How a video is processed
1. The user uploads a video from the Upload page.
2. The tool detects clips of 15 to 60 seconds.
3. Each clip is scored and given 3 suggested tags.
4. The user reviews the report and exports the clips they want.
Processing takes about 1 minute for every 10 minutes of video on Free and Pro plans. Business plan videos use a priority queue and take about 30 seconds for every 10 minutes.

## Exceptions to normal processing
- Exception: videos shorter than 5 minutes are not analysed on any plan.
- Exception: Business customers may ask support for a one-time length extension to 180 minutes for a single video. Approval takes up to 2 business days.

## Recommended action for low scores
Re-title any clip scoring below 55 and add at least 3 relevant tags before publishing. Clips with a strong hook score (85 or above) tend to have higher overall SEO scores.

## How to request a refund
1. Check the refund rules in the current policy (DOC-03).
2. Email billing@cliprank-labs.example from the registered account address.
3. Include the account name, the payment date, and the reason.
4. The billing team replies within 1 business day.

## Support response targets
- Free plan: within 48 hours.
- Pro plan: within 24 hours.
- Business plan: within 4 business hours.

## Maintenance window
The platform is offline for maintenance every Sunday from 02:00 to 03:00 UTC.

## Frequently asked questions
**What export quality do I get?** Free accounts export clips up to 720p. Pro and Business accounts export up to 4K.

**How do I reset my password?** Use "Forgot password" on the login page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes.

**Can I change a clip title after export?** Yes. Edit the title and tags in the report, then export again. Each edit appears in the Activity Log on Business plans.

**Which time zone do activity logs use?** All timestamps are in UTC.

**Is my data shared with other customers?** No. Each account can see only its own videos and reports.
