# ClipRank Labs - Refund, Usage and Data Retention Policy (OLD VERSION)
Document ID: DOC-04 | Policy version: 2.0 | Effective date: 2025-03-01 | End date: 2026-08-31 | Status: ARCHIVED
Superseded by: Version 3.0 (DOC-03). Use this document only when a user asks about old or previous rules.

## 1. Refunds (old rules)
1.1 A customer could request a refund within 7 days of the first payment, provided fewer than 10 clips had been generated.
1.2 There was no special rule for Business annual plans. They followed the same 7-day rule.
1.3 A duplicate charge was refunded only if reported within 30 days.
1.4 Approved refunds were returned within 10 business days.

## 2. Plan limits and prices (old rules)
- Free: 2 videos per month, maximum 20 minutes each.
- Pro: 15 videos per month, maximum 45 minutes each. Price was $15 per month.
- Business: 50 videos per month, maximum 90 minutes each. Price was $49 per month.

## 3. Cancellation (old rule)
Cancellation ended access to paid features on the same day. No access remained for the rest of the paid period.

## 4. Data retention (old rules)
4.1 Uploaded source videos were deleted 30 days after the last activity.
4.2 Generated clips and SEO score reports were kept for 6 months.
4.3 Activity logs were kept for 90 days.
4.4 Personal data was removed within 60 days of account deletion.
