# ClipRank Labs - Refund, Usage and Data Retention Policy
Document ID: DOC-03 | Policy version: 3.0 | Effective date: 2026-09-01 | Status: CURRENT
Replaces: Version 2.0 (archived, see DOC-04)

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains refunds, plan limits, cancellation, and how long ClipRank Labs keeps customer data. It applies to all Free, Pro, and Business accounts. Version 3.0 is the policy to use for any question about current rules.

## 2. Refunds
2.1 General rule: a customer may request a refund within 14 days of the first payment for a paid plan, provided fewer than 20 clips have been generated since that payment.
2.2 Exception A (Business annual plans): a Business annual subscription may be refunded within 30 days of payment. The 20-clip limit does not apply to this exception.
2.3 Exception B (duplicate charges): a duplicate charge is refunded in full at any time within 60 days of the charge, whatever the usage.
2.4 Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 business days.
2.5 Monthly renewals after the first payment are not refundable. A customer should cancel before the renewal date.

## 3. Plan limits
Monthly video allowances and maximum video lengths for each plan are listed in the plan table of the Products document (DOC-02). A video longer than the plan maximum is rejected at upload. Videos shorter than 5 minutes are not analysed on any plan.

## 4. Cancellation and downgrades
A customer can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing. Access to paid features continues until the end of the paid period. A downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing period.

## 5. Data retention
5.1 Uploaded source videos are deleted 90 days after the last activity on that video.
5.2 Generated clips and SEO score reports are kept for 12 months after creation.
5.3 Activity logs (Business plan) are kept for 180 days.
5.4 When an account is deleted, all personal data is removed within 30 days.
