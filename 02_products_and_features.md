## SEO score formula
The SEO Score is a number from 0 to 100: 25% keyword relevance, 20% title strength, 25% hook strength (first 3 seconds), 20% predicted engagement, and 10% thumbnail quality. Ratings: 85 and above is Excellent, 70 to 84 is Good, 55 to 69 is Fair, and below 55 is Needs work.

## Offerings and features
1. Auto Clip Detection: finds moments such as Q&A highlights, big reveals, common mistakes, and step-by-step tips. Detected clips last between 15 and 60 seconds. Available on all plans.
2. SEO Score Report: gives every clip a score from 0 to 100 with a rating. Available on all plans.
3. Hook Analyzer: rates the first 3 seconds of a clip, which carry 25% of the SEO score.
4. Title Strength Checker: scores a clip title and suggests improvements. Title strength carries 20% of the score.
5. Tag Suggestions: proposes 3 suggested tags for each clip, such as "how to" or "beginner guide".
6. Thumbnail Quality Check: rates thumbnail sharpness and clarity. It carries 10% of the score.
7. Engagement Prediction: estimates how viewers will respond. It carries 20% of the score.
8. Keyword Relevance Engine: compares clip content with search keywords. It carries 25% of the score, and Education and Tech videos tend to score highest here.
9. Clip Export: downloads clips as MP4 files. Available on all plans, with the quality limits described in DOC-05.
10. Bulk Export and CSV Reports: exports many clips and score tables at once. Available on Pro and Business only.
11. Team Workspace: lets several people share videos and reports. Business plan only.
12. Activity Log: records logins, exports, downloads, and edits with timestamps. Business plan only.
